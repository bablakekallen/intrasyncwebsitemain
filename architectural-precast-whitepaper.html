Built Inside a PCI-Certified Plant

The plant, Walters & Wolf Precast, was listed by the Precast/Prestressed Concrete Institute as a PCI-Certified plant in two categories: architectural precast (AA) and glass fiber reinforced concrete (G). PCI plant certification is based on audits against PCI's quality control manuals, MNL-117 for architectural precast and MNL-130 for GFRC.

That is where IntraSync's founder, Zachary Frye, built and deployed the first generation of CastLogic, directly on the plant floor and in phases over three years. The plant staffed up to 150 people in its busiest season.

CastLogic is that operational foundation rebuilt as an AI-native cloud ERP.

“I didn't design this from a whiteboard. I built the first version standing on the plant floor, watching where the hours and the panels actually disappeared. CastLogic is that same operational logic, rebuilt on modern tooling with AI in every module.”

Zachary Frye, Founder & CTO, IntraSync Industrial

The Starting Point: Extensive Downtime

Before the system, the plant ran the way many precasters still do, with scheduling, estimating, quality, and maintenance tracked in separate tools and spreadsheets. Downtime was extensive. The plant's own records put numbers on it:

What the Plant Connected

Over the three-year rollout, the system brought the plant's core functions onto one platform:

  • Scheduling & production: mold and labor sequencing moved out of spreadsheets.
  • Quality: defects and finish issues tracked in-process instead of at ship.
  • Estimating: structured, reusable estimates instead of re-keying.
  • Maintenance: planned servicing tracked alongside production.
  • Inventory & yard: material and yard visibility in the same system.

During the same period, annual revenue grew from $28M to about $31M, an increase of roughly $3M (about 11%). Revenue reflects everything happening at the plant over those years, not the software alone.
