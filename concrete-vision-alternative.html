Why Precast Producers Look for a Concrete Vision Alternative

Concrete Vision is operations software, and many plants run it alongside a separate accounting system, a design tool, and a QC tool. The producers we talk to want those pieces in one place, with one record for each job and each piece.

💰

Operations and accounting together

CastLogic Accounting runs on the same data as production. Pours update WIP, the bill of lading triggers billing, and job cost is current without re-keying into a second system.

📐

Designer and QC included

The CastLogic Designer and Verify quality control are part of CastLogic Production, so structure design and inspections are not separate products to license and connect.

🤖

Scheduling that proposes the plan

Plan the schedule by hand, or let ISA propose it around your molds, crews, and materials for a planner to review. A Manual / Automated switch sits on the schedule.

👥

Unlimited seats

No per-user licensing. Put supervisors, QC techs, and yard crews on the system instead of rationing logins to the office.

Concrete Vision Alternative FAQ

What is a good alternative to Concrete Vision for precast concrete producers?

CastLogic by IntraSync Industrial is a cloud-native ERP built specifically for precast concrete manufacturing. It covers estimating, production scheduling, yard, dispatch, and the field, and includes the structure Designer and Verify quality control, with CastLogic Accounting available on the same platform.

Does Concrete Vision include accounting?

Concrete Vision's published module list does not include accounting, a general ledger, or invoicing, and its integrations page does not name an accounting package, as of November 2026. CastLogic Accounting provides the general ledger, AP/AR, project accounting, and real-time job costing on the same data as production.

Does CastLogic replace Stack-It and Idencia for a Concrete Vision plant?

Yes. CastLogic Designer and CastLogic Verify are both included with CastLogic Production. Designer handles structure design, rebar detailing, and submittal packages, and Verify records inspections and quality results against each piece.

Can we keep Tekla, Revit, or ALLPLAN?

Yes. CastLogic integrates with Tekla Structures, Revit, ALLPLAN, and AutoCAD today, so existing detailing workflows carry over.

How long does it take to move from Concrete Vision to CastLogic?

Plan on six months for a CastLogic implementation, including data mapping, configuration, training, and a parallel period before go-live. Implementation is included, and a dedicated project manager runs it with you.

Does CastLogic charge per user?

No. CastLogic is licensed with unlimited seats, so office staff, plant supervisors, QC, and yard crews can all use the system without per-user fees.

Can I keep my existing accounting system?

Yes. Plants that prefer to keep QuickBooks, Sage, NetSuite, SAP, or Oracle can run CastLogic Production as the operational system and integrate with their existing financial system through open APIs.
