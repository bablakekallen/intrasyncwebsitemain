Titan 3000 Migration FAQ

What is a good alternative to Titan 3000 for precast concrete producers?

CastLogic by IntraSync Industrial is a cloud-native ERP built specifically for precast concrete manufacturing. It covers estimating, the structure Designer, production scheduling, QC, yard, dispatch, accounting, and HR in one platform, with a dedicated migration path for producers moving off Titan 3000.

Can I migrate my data out of Titan 3000?

Yes. Our AI-assisted migration tooling reads Titan 3000 database backups and maps structure IDs, product and pipe catalogs, historical jobs, sales orders, BOMs, financials, and payroll mapping into CastLogic, with saveable mapping templates and audited re-imports.

Can accounting data be migrated from Titan 3000?

Yes. Titan 3000 is a full ERP, so a migration can include financial data. We map the legacy chart of accounts to a clean, standardized general ledger and carry over open AP, AR, and purchase orders.

How long does it take to move from Titan 3000 to CastLogic?

A typical implementation takes 4 to 6 months depending on company size and complexity, including data mapping, configuration, training, and parallel testing before go-live.

Does CastLogic charge per user?

No. CastLogic is licensed with unlimited seats, so office staff, plant supervisors, QC, and yard crews can all use the system without per-user fees.

Can I keep my existing accounting system if I leave Titan 3000?

Yes. CastLogic includes a full accounting module, but plants that prefer to keep QuickBooks, Sage, NetSuite, SAP, or Oracle can run CastLogic as the operational system and integrate through open APIs.
