The Power of Design Inside Your ERP

Most precast producers design in one tool, estimate in another, and schedule in a third — re-entering the same structure data at every step. CastLogic Designer lives inside the ERP, so a design is also a takeoff, a BOM, and a production-ready work order.

  • • Design data re-typed into the estimate, then again into production

  • • Submittal packages assembled by hand for every municipality
  • • Separate CAD licenses for everyone who touches a drawing

Complete Structure Design Suite

From the first rim elevation to the final submittal sheet, Designer handles the drawing work your engineers do by hand today.

🧱

Parametric Structure Configurator

Configure manholes, catch basins, inlets, vaults, septic tanks, box culverts, and pull boxes from structure codes. Enter rim and invert elevations and the system computes overall depth, stack composition, and grade-ring requirements automatically.

🗼

Automated Stacking Logic

Auto-builds the structure stack from your product catalog — monobase or standard base — filtering out incompatible components (crane limits, disabled items) and proposing valid alternatives instead of letting errors through.

🩻

Automated Rebar Detailing

Rebar logic is built in: reinforcement detailing and rebar schedules generate with the design — including flat tops and bases — eliminating hours of manual detailing per structure.

📐

Drawing Template Designer

Build your own drawing templates — plan views, dimensions, callouts, weights tables, materials boxes, logos — and group them into multi-sheet packages: submittal, rebar schedule, and hardware schedule. Assign templates per project to enforce standards automatically.

📋

Submittal Packages in One Click

Generate complete, professional submittal packages straight from the design. Clone templates to handle municipal spec variants — Caltrans, county, or city standards — without rebuilding from scratch.

🛡️

Standards-Enforcing Validation

Company-level rules distinguish warnings from blocking errors: wall thicknesses, channel depths, bench and sump rules, grade-ring min/max, joint conventions. Invalid structures can't be finalized — your standards are in the software, not a binder.

🧾

Automatic Bill of Materials

Every design produces its BOM — concrete, steel, lifting hardware, connectors, boots, and seals tied to manufacturer part numbers so hole sizes and components populate themselves. The BOM prices the quote and drives purchasing.

🌎

Metric & Imperial, US & Canada

Dual-unit support with project-level metric override, US industry-standard pipe libraries, and regional compliance handling for both US and Canadian producers.

🗂️

Centralized Product Maintenance

Product, structure, and pipe catalogs are maintained in one place — a single source of truth the Designer reads from. Add one-off custom parts on a single structure or promote them to the shared component library.

⚡ Built for Speed

Keyboard-first field traversal, auto-save with undo, four-sided structure views, and drag-adjustable dimensions. Design a structure in the time it used to take to open the CAD file.

🖨️ Clean Output

Print-ready PDF export for drawings and submittals, with per-face dimension controls and customizable joint rendering to match how your shop reads drawings.

📦 Legacy Data Comes With You

Structure IDs, pipe catalogs, and product data import directly from legacy systems like Titan 3000 — audited for mapping fidelity, re-importable as you refine.
