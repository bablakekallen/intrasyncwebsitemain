The Power of Fully Integrated ERP Accounting

Unlike standalone accounting software, CastLogic Accounting is the financial backbone of your complete ERP system—seamlessly connecting production, inventory, HR, and operations into one unified platform.

❌ Standalone Accounting Software

  • • Manual data entry between systems creates errors and delays
  • • Job costing requires reconciliation across multiple platforms
  • • Inventory values don't sync with financial records
  • • Payroll costs disconnected from project tracking
  • • Real-time profitability analysis is impossible
  • • Month-end close takes days of reconciliation work

✓ CastLogic Integrated ERP Accounting

  • ✓ Automatic data flow from production to financials—zero manual entry
  • ✓ Real-time job costing tracks every labor hour, material, and overhead automatically
  • ✓ Inventory perpetually synced with COGS and financial statements
  • ✓ Payroll integrated directly into project costs and WIP accounting
  • ✓ Live profitability dashboards show margins by project, product line, or customer
  • ✓ Month-end close in hours not days with automated reconciliations

The Bottom Line

CastLogic Accounting isn't just software—it's the financial intelligence layer that connects your entire operation, eliminating data silos and giving you complete visibility from production to profit.
