An Alternative to Elematic Plant Control and ELiPLAN for Precast Producers

November 17, 2026 • 3 min read • By Zachary Frye

Elematic builds production lines for hollow-core slabs, walls, and floors, and sells software to plan and run them. If your plant casts on Elematic equipment, the software question is usually framed as which Elematic modules to buy. There is another question worth asking first: does the software have to be Elematic's at all?

What Elematic Sells on the Software Side

  • Plant Control: Elematic's production planning and control suite, built from modules.
  • FloorMES: the MES for hollow-core floor production.
  • WallMES: the MES for precast wall panel production.
  • ELiPLAN: resource planning, scheduling, and management for precast fabricators.

These are designed around Elematic production lines. That is a real strength on those lines. It also means the software's reach tends to end where the Elematic equipment ends.

The Line Is Hardware. The Software Is a Choice.

What CastLogic Does

CastLogic replaces the software layer on Elematic machinery. The hardware integrates into the CastLogic platform, which covers production planning, line execution, and resource planning in one system.

Running Elematic equipment does not commit a plant to Elematic software. The extruder, the saw, and the wall line need production data and they report status. CastLogic supplies the one and collects the other.

How the Roles Map Across

  • Bed and line planning: CastLogic Schedule, planned by hand or proposed by ISA around your plant's limits.
  • Line execution: CastLogic Flow releases work and tracks each piece from cast to yard.
  • Quality control: CastLogic Verify records inspections against the same piece record.
  • Resource planning and the business side: estimating, yard, dispatch, and accounting in the same platform.

Why This Matters for Plants That Cast More Than One Thing

Many producers with a hollow-core line also cast walls, stairs, columns, beams, or utility products on forms that no line-specific MES was built for. They end up with one system for the automated line and spreadsheets for everything else.

CastLogic handles hollow-core, walls, structural, architectural, utility, and pipe in one system, on any mix of machinery. The delivery schedule sees every piece on the job, not only the ones that came off the automated line.

Design Data

Elematic's tools take product data from design systems such as Tekla Structures. CastLogic does as well: Tekla Structures, Revit, ALLPLAN, and AutoCAD integrate today, and CastLogic Designer designs underground and utility structures inside the platform. The design record is the production record.

Questions to Ask

  • Which of our products and forms fall outside the vendor MES?
  • How many separate software modules will we license and maintain?
  • Where do estimating, dispatch, and accounting live, and how do they get production data?
  • How is it licensed, and what does adding users cost?
  • Can the line accept production data from another system?

Plants that want to keep Plant Control for now can. CastLogic integrates with any MES and any machinery, so connecting first and replacing later is a normal path.
