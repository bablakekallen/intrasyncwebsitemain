Concrete Vision vs CastLogic: A Module-by-Module Comparison

November 24, 2026 • 5 min read • By Zachary Frye

Concrete Vision and CastLogic are both built for precast, and they overlap across most of the plant. This comparison goes module by module, using what Concrete Vision publishes on its own site, so you can see where the two line up and where they differ.

The Short Version

Concrete Vision is precast operations software: contacts and quoting through production, yard, dispatch, and the field. CastLogic covers the same operations and adds the pieces Concrete Vision's site assigns to other products or does not list: structure design, accounting, and AI.

Where the Two Are Close

Both systems are web-based and built around the precast piece. Both schedule production against beds, track pieces through the yard, plan loads, and follow the job into the field. Both bring design data in from Tekla Structures, Revit, and ALLPLAN. A plant moving from one to the other will recognize the workflow.

Where They Differ

Accounting. Concrete Vision's module list stops at operations. CastLogic Accounting sits on the same data as production, so pours update WIP, shipments trigger billing, and job cost is current without a second system. Plants that want to keep their accounting system can: CastLogic integrates with QuickBooks, Sage, NetSuite, SAP, and Oracle.

Design. Concrete Vision lists Stack-It as an integration. CastLogic Designer is part of the platform, so a manhole or vault configured for a quote is the same record that gets scheduled and poured.

Quality control. Concrete Vision tracks NCRs and post-pour work and lists outside tools for testing and product tracking. CastLogic Verify is included with Production and records inspections against the piece.

Scheduling. Concrete Vision describes drag-and-drop scheduling. CastLogic Schedule offers the same hands-on planning, and ISA can propose the schedule for a planner to review.

Pricing. Concrete Vision does not publish pricing; its FAQ says it depends on factors including the number of user licenses. CastLogic is priced with unlimited seats.

Which Fits

  • If your operations run well and accounting, design, and QC already live comfortably in other tools, the difference will feel smaller.
  • If month-end depends on re-keying production into the books, or you are paying for several products to cover one job, a single platform changes the math.
  • If planners spend their mornings rebuilding the schedule by hand, look closely at automated scheduling.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Concrete Vision and CastLogic?

Both are built for precast producers and cover estimating, scheduling, yard, dispatch, and the field. CastLogic also includes a structure Designer, Verify quality control, AI scheduling, and an accounting product on the same platform. Concrete Vision's published modules do not list accounting or AI, as of November 2026.

Does CastLogic replace Stack-It?

Yes. CastLogic Designer is included with CastLogic Production and handles parametric structure design with auto-stacking, rebar detailing, and submittal packages.

Does CastLogic replace Idencia for a Concrete Vision plant?

CastLogic Verify is included with CastLogic Production and records inspections and quality results against each piece, so a separate QC tracking tool is not required.

Do both systems work with Tekla, Revit, and ALLPLAN?

Yes. Concrete Vision lists all three as engineering integrations, and CastLogic integrates with Tekla Structures, Revit, ALLPLAN, and AutoCAD today.
