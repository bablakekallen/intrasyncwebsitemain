Payroll, HR & Time

  • ✓Everee — embedded payroll processing with journal entries flowing back into the CastLogic general ledger, including daily-pay options
  • ✓Paylocity — full payroll integration for plants running Paylocity
  • ✓TimeClock Plus (TCP) — consolidate multiple TCP accounts, import time with leave and overtime, persistent employee mapping
  • ✓BambooHR — time and job-code data flowing into production and job costing
  • ✓Benefits Navigator — automated benefits administration and reporting
💰

Enterprise Financial Systems

Owned by a group that standardizes on a corporate ERP? CastLogic runs the plant as the operational system of record and feeds the corporate ledger.

  • ✓NetSuite — operational data, billing milestones, and invoicing flows
  • ✓SAP & Oracle — open-API integration with enterprise financial platforms
  • ✓QuickBooks & Sage — for plants keeping their existing small-business accounting
  • ✓IFS Cloud — bi-directional operation status sync for enterprise manufacturers
  • ✓Any open-API system — switchable connectors architecture; if your financial platform exposes an API, we integrate with it
🏦

Banking & Payments

  • ✓Bank feeds — automatic transaction import and reconciliation with real-time balances
  • ✓Corporate card imports — card transaction data (e.g., Ramp) landing directly in AP
  • ✓Online payment portal — let customers pay invoices online instead of mailing checks
  • ✓Point-of-sale options — walk-in and consignment sales support (e.g., Square)

CAD, BIM & Engineering

  • ✓Revit — import full BIM data including bills of material and component metadata
  • ✓AutoCAD & SolidWorks — design aggregation into unified project dashboards for estimating and production handoff
  • ✓ML drawing extraction — machine-vision takeoffs pulling dimensions, volumes, weights, and penetrations from PDF drawings
  • ✓Engineering plugin roadmap — Tekla and expanded BIM tooling via DesignLogic

Email, Calendar & Communication

  • ✓Outlook & Gmail — email and calendar sync tied to the CRM and sales pipeline
  • ✓Notifications — push, email, and SMS alerts for schedule changes, QC holds, inventory minimums, and weather
  • ✓Automated summaries — scheduled daily inventory and status emails to the people who need them

Legacy ERP Data Migration

Switching systems shouldn't mean losing your history. Our AI-assisted migration tooling reads legacy database backups and maps them into CastLogic.

  • ✓Titan 3000 — structure IDs, product and pipe catalogs, historical jobs, sales orders, BOMs, financials, and payroll mapping
  • ✓AI-assisted schema mapping — fuzzy matching with saveable mapping templates, data transformations, and audited re-imports
  • ✓GL standardization — map legacy charts of accounts to a clean, standardized GL with open AP/AR/PO carryover

Don't See Your System?

CastLogic is built on a modern, API-first architecture with switchable connectors. If the system you rely on exposes an open API — ERP, CRM, bid platform, or financial software — we can build the connection as part of your implementation.
