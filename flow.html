Built for Precast Production

Production tracking challenges unique to precast manufacturing require specialized solutions -- from robotic pour stations to automated curing chambers.

Pour & Cure Tracking: Concrete batch tracking by component, pour time and temperature logs from robotic pour stations, automated cure cycle monitoring via IoT sensors in curing chambers, and strength test integration.
Pallet Circulation Tracking: Real-time pallet position tracking, station-by-station progress monitoring, automated cycle time capture from Vollert and Elematic circulation systems, and throughput optimization.
Prestress Operations: Strand tensioning records, release sequence tracking, elongation measurements, and transfer date documentation with data captured from automated stressing equipment.
Form & Mold Management: Setup time tracking, mold cleaning verification, damage reporting, and multi-cavity bed management with integration to automated formwork systems.
Finishing Operations: Surface treatment tracking, embedment installation, grouting operations, and patch and repair documentation across manual and robotic finishing stations.
Environmental Monitoring: Plant temperature and humidity logs from IoT sensors, automated curing chamber condition tracking, and weather impact analysis on outdoor operations.

Complete Certification Traceability

Every production record feeds into PCI audit documentation and EPD data collection. Batch records, cure data, and energy usage are automatically captured for compliance reporting -- including automated data from robotic equipment.
