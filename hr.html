Built for Manufacturing Workforce Complexity

Generic ERPs lose the nuance of how manufacturing labor actually gets paid. CastLogic preserves that nuance through the integration—so prevailing wage, shift differentials, and skill-based pay reach the GL with the right context.

Complex Pay Scenarios

  • Prevailing Wage Context: Davis-Bacon and state prevailing wage data from your HR platform flows into ERP project costing and reporting
  • Shift Differentials: Day/swing/night rate context preserved end-to-end from your HR platform through to job cost
  • Skill-Based Pay: Certification, role, and skill data synced from HR drives the right pay rate to the right job
  • Production Bonuses: Incentive pay based on output, quality, or safety metrics

Additional Manufacturing Capabilities

Multi-Site Posting
